# Explore Rainwater Management Solutions

Discover which of five nature-based solutions could be feasible on your property, based on your building type, garden layout, and local soil and slope conditions. This page covers the typologies assessed in the underlying study.

<h2 class="solution-category">Building Based Solutions</h2>

<div class="solution-block">
  <div class="solution-name">Green Roof</div>
  <div class="solution-desc">A vegetated roof layer that absorbs and slows rainfall before it reaches the ground.</div>
  <ul class="solution-points">
    <li>Depends mainly on roof pitch and available roof area, not soil conditions.</li>
    <li>Also helps cool your home in summer and can extend your roof's lifespan and increase the value of the plot.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="solution-area">Applicable area: building rooftops</div>
</div>

<div class="solution-block">
  <div class="solution-name">Rainwater Cistern</div>
  <div class="solution-desc">Stores roof runoff for garden use or non-potable household use, disconnecting your roof from the sewer.</div>
  <ul class="solution-points">
    <li>Depends on having enough contributing roof area; suitable across most building types.</li>
    <li>Lowers your water bill and can qualify for a stormwater fee reduction.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="solution-area">Applicable area: connected to roof downpipes, placed adjacent to the building</div>
</div>

<h2 class="solution-category">Ground Based Solutions</h2>

<div class="solution-block">
  <div class="solution-name">Rain Garden</div>
  <div class="solution-desc">A shallow, planted depression that captures and infiltrates runoff from roofs and paved areas.</div>
  <ul class="solution-points">
    <li>Needs a reasonably open, contiguous patch of garden with suitable soil and gentle slope.</li>
    <li>Low cost to build and easy to maintain once planted.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="solution-area">Applicable area: private yards, near downspouts and setback areas</div>
</div>

<div class="solution-block">
  <div class="solution-name">Infiltration Trench</div>
  <div class="solution-desc">A gravel-filled trench that lets water soak into the ground along a narrow strip.</div>
  <ul class="solution-points">
    <li>Fits narrow side or rear yard strips, often suited to terraced housing.</li>
    <li>Works well where garden space is limited but a thin edge is available.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="solution-area">Applicable area: side yards, rear boundary strips, narrow unpaved gaps between structures</div>
</div>

<div class="solution-block">
  <div class="solution-name">Bioswale</div>
  <div class="solution-desc">A vegetated channel with an engineered soil layer that conveys, filters, and partly infiltrates runoff.</div>
  <ul class="solution-points">
    <li>Needs open space along a driveway, boundary, or slope with concentrated flow.</li>
    <li>Good for managing runoff along a sloped or heavily paved part of your property.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="solution-area">Applicable area: beside driveways, along property edges, near parking areas, sloped sections</div>
</div>

<p class="solutions-footnote">Each solution's feasibility on your specific parcel depends on site conditions such as soil infiltration capacity, slope, groundwater depth, and available space, not on preference alone.</p>
